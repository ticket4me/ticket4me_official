Why T4M

Built different

No hidden fees. No bots. No nonsense.

01

Account-bound tickets

Every ticket is tied to a verified T4M account. No screenshots, no fakes, no resale on shady third-party sites.

02

Fair resale cap

Resale is allowed on T4M only, capped at 75% above face value. Fans get fair prices, sellers still make a profit.

03

Presale codes

Venues and artists can issue exclusive presale codes to fan clubs, newsletter subscribers, or loyal buyers.

04

Pro + Indie venues

Pro venues get the full CTS dashboard and scanner. Indie venues get simple, free tools to get started fast.

05

Instant payouts

Venues receive payouts via Stripe within 2 business days of an event selling out or going live.

06

US-first platform

Built specifically for the US market. No international clutter — just clean, fast ticketing for American events.
